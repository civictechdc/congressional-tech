Reviewed categories across catalog versions
===========================================
Date: 2026-10-18. Supersedes the "repeat this rebind for every public catalog
replacement" step in REVIEW_CATALOG_REBIND.txt for the public site.

Problem
-------
A review publication (reviews.json, pinned by review_sha256) records row
positions for the one catalog it was exported from, and the site loaded it
only when the live catalog_id matched. The public catalog was replaced 1-3
times a day (17 generations between 2026-10-04 and 2026-10-17), so reviewed
search was dark most of the time and every replacement needed a manual rebind,
a release upload and a Pages deploy.

Design
------
- apps/site/document-review-identities.json records each review's stable
  identity for review publication fc5d361e: document_id, member source_ids and
  stored body_key (86 reviews, 286 sources). It was generated by
  apps/site/scripts/filename_review_binding.py from reviews.json and its exact
  catalog 76758ea7, and refuses to write unless every review binds back to its
  own positions.
- Every viewer publication (publish-filename-viewer.py, used by the R2 workflow
  and the recovery dispatcher) writes viewer-v2/review-binding.json: this
  version's positions for each review whose document, members and body are
  unchanged, and a reason for each withdrawn review (document_missing,
  sources_changed, body_changed). It is listed with its sha256 in the release
  manifest.
- The site (filename-reviews.mjs) uses the binding when the release has one:
  it requires the binding's review_sha256 to equal the committed descriptor's
  and its catalog_id to equal the live catalog, then places only bound reviews.
  Withdrawn reviews show the original categories. Releases without a binding
  keep the old exact-catalog rule.

Evidence
--------
Identities generated from catalog 76758ea7 bind on other real catalogs:
- d42b9f66 (the original review catalog): 86/86 bound, and every group and
  source position equals the originally published, fully admitted positions.
- 407b6cce (checkpoint 16) and 16b8f0e1 (checkpoint 19): 86/86 bound.
- 44f57627 (a one-row integration catalog): 0/86 bound, all document_missing.
Across d42b9f66 -> 76758ea7 all 86 document_ids were stable while all 86
positions moved.

Limits
------
The binding checks identity (same document, sources and stored body), not the
review's content. A changed body withdraws its review; a new review or a
changed review still needs a new review publication and a regenerated identity
file. Document identities held across catalogs built with house-naming 3.0.0
and 3.1.0; the 3.2.7 re-baseline is expected to keep them too, but no 3.2.7
catalog has been observed yet. Check its binding counts in the publish log.

Live result
-----------
2026-10-18: deployed in 43b6ce2. The daily capture (finished 11:11 UTC)
selected catalog 90001c4ced354875b950e9b19051482b, replacing 76758ea7. The R2
publisher logged "Review binding: 86 bound, 0 withdrawn"; the published
review-binding.json matches its manifest sha256, the site's review pin and
the live catalog. The site's own loadReviews against the live data reports
reviewed search on with 81 applied groups and no error. Under the previous
exact-catalog rule, reviewed search would have gone dark at this replacement.
