Disclosure and biography alias conflicts: retained-body review, part 1
====================================================================

Decision
--------
Keep the filename claims separate from observations about actual contents.
Do not collapse all disclosure/biography groups into either kind. The reviewed
population includes disclosure-only forms, biography-only files and genuine
combined documents. A shared eligible body proves that aliases identify the
same saved bytes; it does not prove that every alias correctly names the contents.

Evidence reviewed
-----------------
Selected the 38 pending disclosure/biography groups from review-evidence-v4,
using its committed checkpoint and exact ledger hash. Reused the maintained
eligible-body policy and excluded IDs and all retained keys from all 400 frozen
pilot records before access. Each group had exactly one eligible body. All 38
public saved-copy responses matched their expected SHA-256 and PDF signature.
No publisher acquisition, pilot inspection, OCR or production mutation occurred.

The fetched set contains 279 pages. Completed all-page visual review of the
24 single-page PDFs and five two-page PDFs: 29 documents and 34 pages total.
The JSON checkpoint records exact body, text and render hashes for each review.
Native text supplemented visual inspection; the review does not transcribe or
re-publish contact details, signatures or text hidden by visible redactions.

Observed forms in the 29 completed reviews:
- 23 disclosure forms without a biography or CV in the exact retained PDF.
- Five disclosure forms with substantive biography material, either embedded
  in the form's CV/resume answer or attached as a separate page.
- One two-page resume without a disclosure form.

These counts describe this reviewed subset, not an estimated rate for all
unclassified filenames or an independently labeled evaluation population.

Important boundaries
--------------------
A disclosure form's request to attach a CV is not evidence that the saved PDF
contains one. The Lyle form even says Attached, but its exact retained PDF has
only the form page. Short job titles and representational-capacity answers do
not by themselves constitute a separate biographical document.

The Koenig form uses BIO for Biotechnology Industry Organization. The filename's
bio token can therefore be an organization abbreviation rather than a biography.
The Kitcheyan file has disclosure in its filename but contains only a resume.
These examples rule out a universal precedence rule based on either word.

The Sweeney, Eberhart and O'Toole forms include substantive career narratives
inside the form. Lunny and Williams have separate biography pages. Preserve
multiple document forms when the complete contents support them.

Implementation implications
---------------------------
No house_naming runtime change is justified by this batch. Its original filename
claims remain useful source evidence even where the contents disagree. Any later
content-based resolution must bind the exact eligible body and distinguish
complete-packet review from a heading or first-page hint. Preserve the original
filename claims and the reason for overriding or retaining them.

The frozen PDF candidate remains unchanged. These assistant findings are not
independent references and cannot unlock held-out evaluation. The current
metadata ledger still has 91 pending groups; this separate body-review receipt
does not silently mark them approved or change the catalog.
