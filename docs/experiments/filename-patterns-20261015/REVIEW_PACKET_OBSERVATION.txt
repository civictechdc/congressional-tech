Complete packet observations in local review bundles: self-review
=================================================================
Findings
--------
No blocking finding in the representation or reader checks. The prior compiled
review retained form names and a receipt locator but omitted page sections,
notes, source discrepancies and completeness observations. Version 2 carries the
entire validated receipt record and requires exact equality with its bound source
before exposing a candidate. This makes the five unsupported packets explainable
without changing selected kinds or discarding unsupported components.

Compatibility is explicit: version 1 bundles must be regenerated through admission.
Existing files remain intact. The low-level selector still accepts prior compiled
inputs; the package writer always emits complete observations in version 2. This
is local evidence representation, not an independent rereading or public release.

Data flow and invariants
------------------------
H1: Projecting a small list of page-related fields is sufficient. Refined after
reading all five packet records: Simmons also has source_completeness_observations,
while funding tables and proposed rules carry critical distinctions in observations.
Copying the complete validated record preserves both known and future fields and
avoids a second lossy field mapping. List order and literal strings remain intact.
Primary/component roles remain in the existing compiled fields; source filenames,
claims and selected kinds remain separate on the baseline document.

H2: An embedded observation alone establishes its provenance. Refuted: the reader
first requires the receipt path/hash in the admission bindings, then matches the
entire object to the exact document's record in that receipt. Duplicate and absent
records are refused. Body identity and form set must agree with the compiled review.
All bound files and the implementation are rechecked before returning candidates.
A receipt is parsed once per load; each observation is copied in the detached result.

H3: Describing unsupported forms requires selecting their supported subset.
Refuted: the existing annotation status already preserves the complete packet.
The new representation supplies its evidence without changing that status. In
particular, disclosure-role filenames do not turn funding tables into signed
questionnaires; proposed replacement rules do not imply adopted rules; a complete
retained-PDF review does not establish completeness of original tax filings.

The admission, bundle, selector and import-fence suite passes 94 tests in 1.36s.

Actual cohort check
-------------------
packet-observation-v1 admits and consumes version 2 directly on the complete saved
catalog pair. All 86 observations / 530 reviewed pages equal their receipt records
exactly. Removing only the new body_observation field restores every prior candidate
value, including selected kinds, group bindings, roles and statuses. The result
remains 81 selected candidates and five whole-packet annotations; all withdrawal
checks pass on complete source/document rows with matching full-catalog digests.
Both entire catalog files, nine frozen sources and the pilot/labels remain unchanged.

The five unsupported packets retain 223 pages across 44 recorded sections: grant
awards 9 pages, expenditure schedule 70, contract awards 8, proposed rules 17 and
Simmons 119. Embedded same-page forms and all source-completeness notes are retained.
The complete candidate file grows from 3,027,282 to 3,251,447 bytes
(224,165 additional bytes) to carry the observations. No performance improvement
is claimed. Admission took 83.523s, consumption 60.164s, and the complete
run 145.467s with peak process RSS 2,275,540,992 bytes on macOS. These are
single-run diagnostics with a different measurement scope from the earlier runs.
The package implementation and reader hashes, tests, input bindings and exact
outputs are pinned in packet_observation_checkpoint_v1.json.

Limits and next steps
---------------------
The embedded record intentionally retains historical local paths, including render
and native-text references. It is not portable yet. Next define local export and
resolution of content-addressed evidence while preserving original locator text,
then specify loading once per catalog/review snapshot and explicit refusal or
withdrawal for consumers. Validate relocation and altered/missing evidence before
viewer integration. Do not silently use a refused bundle as if no review existed.
Independent reference review, held-out evaluation, classification refresh and
publication remain separate requirements.
