Missing-ID investigation
------------------------
Scanned document_id, body_keys, source_url and source_urls across the complete
historical document table. Matched each missing record by exact retained-body
digest and exact URL, without normalizing URLs or using filename similarity.
Rehashed the document table after the scan and verified it still matched the
selected input checksum.

- 66 missing IDs share an exact URL with a historical document group, but none
  shares the frozen nonempty body. Of those 66 matching groups, 65 have no body
  keys and one has only the empty-byte digest.
- 79 missing IDs have neither an exact nonempty body match nor an exact URL
  match. This proves absence from this selected table, not absence from remote
  storage or the forthcoming catalog.
- No nonempty frozen body was found under a different document ID. There is no
  evidence here for automatically remapping the frozen IDs.

The first diagnostic treated any body-key digest as a match. D015's retained
empty-response digest then matched thousands of unrelated rows. The refined
analysis excludes SHA-256 of empty bytes from document-identity evidence and
records 5,034 discarded empty-only matches. D015's one exact-URL match is kept,
with the empty digest visible. Both diagnostic versions remain in local output;
the committed result is the refined version. No reference, source table,
production identity rule or eligibility decision was changed.

Interpretation and next action
------------------------------
This historical baseline can test the full-pair comparison machinery. It cannot
stand in for the frozen pilot catalog or the new verified publication. The
observed differences concern input coverage and retained-body evidence, not
classifier accuracy. The active replay compares this exact historical pair with
its own derived result, so these already-absent pilot IDs are not replay losses.

Finish the active replay and inspect every identity, role, evidence and prior-kind
change. Once publication completes, verify its exact selected pair, rerun the
400-ID reconciliation, and investigate any remaining missing IDs against that
pair. Preserve ineligible records and split membership. Do not substitute URL
matches for exact body identity or unlock held-out review from historical results.
