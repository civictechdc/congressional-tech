Vote action and oversight-plan review
====================================

Patch summary
-------------
The 64-row grouping CSV was read in full again, including all available samples
(up to three per group). It exposed 18 report-to-vote/report transitions and six
oversight-plan-to-vote/plan transitions. A vote's action to report a measure and

extra document kinds. This is filename interpretation, not proof of PDF content.

publisher rule or content reader is introduced. Package, guide, schema and

Findings
--------
Resolved: a report about votes must retain its primary report kind, so adding
report to vote-target refinement alone would be unsafe. The topic-connector
selection and reverse-direction controls cover that case.
Resolved: nested same-kind labels must use the complete phrase for coordination.
The selector now reuses its existing longest-phrase rule for this case.
Limitation: ambiguous combined files, reused URLs and body identity differences
still need retained-content adjudication. Full-catalog qualification and the
held-out evaluation remain separate from this local historical cohort.
