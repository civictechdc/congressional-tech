Explicit PDF content rules: development candidate
=================================================

The candidate adds three opening-page recognizers to congress_api:

* House published hearings require a short uppercase cover title, HEARING /
  BEFORE THE, chamber and committee headings, a serial, a printing statement
  and a Government Publishing/Printing Office imprint. Known imprint lines
  may precede the title because PDF readers can reorder extracted text.
  A House serial does not become an invented GPO citation.
* Amendments require a drafting heading identifying the target, an offered-by
  statement (or explicit proposed amendment to a Senate committee motion),
  and operative editing instructions. Only known drafting metadata may precede
  the heading. Summaries and suggestions to change policy do not qualify.
* Committee vote sheets require a committee heading, roll-call identification,
  vote columns, named members and recorded marks. Blank templates and a roll
  call without committee identification remain unclassified by this rule.

The recognizers preserve existing readings and the restricted second-page
transcript fallback. OCR remains an experiment input; production uses native
PDF text only. No document kinds were added to the taxonomy. Filename rules
remain in house_naming; these content rules belong in congress_api.

Development evidence
--------------------
Pilot checkpoint: 38f7557, with 140 assistant-reviewed development labels.
The reference labels and the 200 held-out selections were not changed.

Input/reader                           Assigned   Agreement   Abstained
Checkpoint production PDF reader            10          10         130
Candidate production PDF reader             24          24         116
Candidate on saved Poppler text              24          24         116
Candidate on OCR-assisted text               27          27         113

The actual PDF comparison invokes read_document_file, including its maintained
small-body/large-file dispatch. Each record is read in a bounded subprocess.
Both baseline and candidate completed without reader errors on these 140 PDFs.
All 10 earlier assignments survive unchanged. The 14 additions are seven
amendments, three House published hearings and four committee vote sheets.

The original saved-text baseline assigned nine records, whereas the actual
PDF reader assigns ten. Native text extraction methods differ: the candidate
finds one more transcript and one fewer amendment through the actual PDF path
than through saved Poppler text. Equal totals do not imply identical records.
The per-record comparison preserves these differences.

This is development evidence, not held-out accuracy or full-catalog coverage.
Reference labels have not received independent human validation. Small counts
per kind cannot establish population-wide precision. Many of the 116 remaining
abstentions are outside the three forms addressed by this candidate.

Validation and retained evidence
--------------------------------
Tests include 26 saved development-page fixtures, missing-marker and quoted-
form contrasts, blank vote templates, second-page exclusions and three actual
PDF cover fixtures. Both the byte and file readers recognize those three PDFs;
their content kinds and document IDs survive paired catalog writes.

The capture workflow now runs these regression tests and triggers when their
fixtures change. The final focused reader suite passes 175 tests. Full offline
suite results are recorded in the candidate output and candidate freeze record.

The v1, v2 and v3 output directories preserve successive development runs.
The final candidate is v3. A v2 guard rejected printing footers emitted before
a hearing title; the real-file regression caught this. The final rule accepts
those specific imprint lines while rejecting prose introducing a quoted cover.
Candidate and evaluator source hashes identify each run; v1/v2 source copies
are retained beside their readings. No held-out text informed these changes.

Evaluation and release gates
----------------------------
The candidate freeze records its code, inputs, tests and development results.
Before held-out evaluation, reconcile every selected ID against the completed
3.1.0 catalog, preserving all changed eligibility and acquisition failures.
Assign held-out reference labels without seeing candidate predictions, then
compare the frozen code. Do not tune on those outcomes.

Report per-kind assignments, agreements, disagreements, abstentions and sample
sizes, with uncertainty intervals. Independently review candidate positives
and ambiguous references before release. Any false positive or unexplained
prior-kind regression blocks the affected rule. Zero or very small per-kind
counts remain insufficient evidence for broad automatic promotion; collect a
separate review set rather than repurposing the held-out set for development.

Publication and retained-body integration are pending. The active 3.1.0 rebuild
has not completed, so the full-catalog 3.2.0 filename replay remains pending too.
The PDF reader has a source fingerprint rather than a separate parser version;
its changed code produces a new body-evidence fingerprint automatically.

Existing saved body readings survive code changes. A later approved rollout
must explicitly reinspect selected retained URLs using the maintained
--reinspect-retained --urls path, or supply validated body readings to catalog
rebuild. Preserve old readings and verify both catalog tables after refresh.
Do not assume that an ordinary rebuild applies new PDF rules to stored bodies.
