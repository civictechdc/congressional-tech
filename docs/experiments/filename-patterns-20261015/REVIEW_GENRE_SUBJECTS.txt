Primary genre and trailing subject review
========================================

Patch summary
-------------
house_naming 3.2.4 separates a document's leading genre from kinds mentioned in
its subject. Testimony-Sheehan-Dem-Memo-... remains testimony; FP-Opening-Memo-...
Progress-Report remains an opening statement. A statement, letter or revenue
estimate explicitly about an amendment keeps the amendment wording without
claiming amendment as its own genre. This refines the shared category selector;
there is no new parser, publisher matcher or content classifier.

Function trace
--------------
Engine.extract (engine.py:60) supplies filename, publisher-qualified observations
and literal spans. filename_metadata (values.py:47) calls the selector and emits
selected kinds while retaining literal field values. _subject_remainders
(extraction.py:554) uses the same selector for recognized subject labels.
document_category_fields (categories.py:71) returns existing field references;
its callers can select kinds without mutating observations or reading bodies.

Data flow and invariants
------------------------
At categories.py:93-96, complete stem observations with a statement/testimony
label identify an existing subject span. The label must precede that span.
At :97-101, only a publisher-qualified memo or opening field at position zero
introduces a publisher title; an interior memo occurrence cannot govern a file.
At :110-115, nested genres are excluded from declared subjects. Publisher titles
use a narrower set of topic kinds. Direct and/&/with coordination and explicit
attachment/appendix/exhibit labels survive this filter. Opening Remarks and
Witness List are not discarded merely because they follow a publisher prefix.
The existing topic-connector rule also covers legislative kinds, so 'Statement
on Amendment' keeps statement while preserving the literal amendment field.
The rule still acts on one filename at a time, not across different aliases.

Test behavior and edge cases
----------------------------
test_reviewed_house_publishers.py:224 covers twelve observed layouts, selected
kinds, retained topic observations, exact filename reconstruction and source
spans. :247 covers explicit components, bare witness-list/remarks labels,
attachments, and operative amendments. :253 covers unrelated/spoofed publishers.
Eleven new cases failed before implementation. A scan of all 7,526 source rows
initially found 23 changed source rows. Review caught an overbroad prefix reading
that removed explicit Opening Remarks. Two additional controls failed against
that candidate; the final rule preserves both opening-remarks and witness-list
labels and confines publisher-title filtering to established topic kinds.
The broad initial candidate passed 5,823 workflow checks, illustrating why its
actual cohort changes still needed review. The final candidate passes all 5,826
offline workflow checks in 111.72 seconds. The separate replay tests pass all 17
checks; the catalog builder verifies all three JSON artifacts. All nine frozen
PDF candidate source hashes remain unchanged.

Findings and remaining limits
-----------------------------
Resolved: a publisher prefix alone is weaker than a declared subject span and
must not remove every trailing category. The narrowed filter and new controls
preserve independent labels. Literal evidence remains available for later review.
Unresolved: four amendment/disclosure/statement cases need exact retained body
review. Their document IDs and body keys do not overlap any of the frozen 400
pilot records. All four exact archive-object reads were denied by the configured
credentials; no body bytes were read, and no publisher was visited. Missing
access is not evidence that the retained objects are absent or that a filename
reading is correct. Retain the failure records and do not retry without changed
access evidence. See genre_subject_checkpoint.json for the selected IDs/keys.
This bounded historical cohort does not establish population precision or qualify
the pending publication. Independent held-out references and the frozen native
PDF evaluation remain separate requirements.

Complete-group replay and verdict
---------------------------------
The final 3.2.3-to-3.2.4 replay covers all 7,526 sources and 1,949 complete groups
in 222.02 seconds. It changes 22 source kinds and 17 group kinds. Every change
removes only extra topic kinds; all changed rows were read in full. All IDs,
group membership, roles, protected evidence, kind provenance and representative
filenames remain unchanged. Input bytes and bound implementation hashes match.
The replay reads no bodies.

The review was regenerated against the original 3.0.0 cohort. All 60 transition
rows, with up to three samples each, were read in full. Its 1,903 changed-document
ledger remains pending individual adjudication. This is not a claim to have
reviewed the contents of every document or measured classification precision.

VERDICT: APPROVE the bounded local filename repair.
Coverage: ADEQUATE for the changed selection paths. Confidence: HIGH in the
observed preservation results and bounded filename behavior. The broad catalog,
independent reference and publication gates remain open.
