Correction to the review plan
-----------------------------
Multiple retained body keys do not necessarily mean multiple usable documents.
The current 3.2.5 ledger's 1,903 changed groups contain 1,821 groups with exactly
one body key eligible under the maintained document-body policy and 82 with none.
No group has more than one eligible key. Retain all capture histories, including
the multiple_retained_bodies flags, but do not describe those flags as evidence
of two distinct usable documents.

Among the 109 pending groups, 103 have one eligible body key and six have none.
All 15 explicit TEXT vote groups pair an eligible PDF/200 document capture with
an HTML/404 error response. The 23 pending groups flagged with multiple retained
bodies therefore need review of one eligible body each, not two document bodies.
The six disclosure/biography groups with multiple retained keys likewise each
have only one eligible key. This changes the content-review queue, not the
filenames, classification claims or retained capture evidence.
