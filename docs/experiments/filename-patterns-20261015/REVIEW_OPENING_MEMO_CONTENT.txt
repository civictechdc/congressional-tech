Opening-statement and memorandum content review, part 1
======================================================
Reviewed all 34 retained pages in nine opening-statement packets and the
12-page TSCA memorandum packet. The additive opening_memo_content_review_v1.json
receipt binds the original ledger, selection, retained PDFs, extracted text
and every inspected page render. It follows the normalized VE-letter receipt.

Results
-------
All nine statement packets contain opening remarks. Seven stand alone as
prepared remarks or released statements; two have a news-release introduction

both opening-statement and press-release observations. Press-office contact

page contains only a communications contact line. The Miller WIA native text
has fragmented reading order on page 1; the rendered page resolves that order.
References to reports, amendments, witness testimony or letters do not mean
those documents are attached.

The TSCA packet includes a May 13, 2015 markup memorandum on pages 1-4 and its
attached April 13 hearing memorandum on pages 5-12. Pagination restarts at the
attachment. The final page includes two panels of expected witnesses. Preserve
the committee-memorandum and witness-list forms, both memo dates, and the
attachment relationship. The cited discussion draft and testimony are not
included as separate documents in this packet.
