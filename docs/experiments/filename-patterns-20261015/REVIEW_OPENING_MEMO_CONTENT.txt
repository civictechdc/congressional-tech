Opening-statement and memorandum content review, part 1
======================================================
Reviewed all 34 retained pages in nine opening-statement packets and the
12-page TSCA memorandum packet. The additive opening_memo_content_review_v1.json
receipt binds the original ledger, selection, retained PDFs, extracted text
and every inspected page render. It follows the normalized VE-letter receipt.

Results
-------
All nine statement packets contain opening remarks. Seven stand alone as
prepared remarks or released statements; two have a news-release introduction
and reproduce the remarks (Pallone CHIP/CHC and Miller WIA). The latter retain
both opening-statement and press-release observations. Press-office contact
information or a PRESS filename token alone was not treated as proof of a
separate press-release form. These are content observations, not new runtime
classifications or claims that the remarks were actually delivered.

Speier's two pages and Cummings's three-page McPherson Square packet have no
native extracted text. All were visually read without OCR. The third Cummings
page contains only a communications contact line. The Miller WIA native text
has fragmented reading order on page 1; the rendered page resolves that order.
References to reports, amendments, witness testimony or letters do not mean
those documents are attached.

The TSCA packet includes a May 13, 2015 markup memorandum on pages 1-4 and its
attached April 13 hearing memorandum on pages 5-12. Pagination restarts at the
attachment. The final page includes two panels of expected witnesses. Preserve
the committee-memorandum and witness-list forms, both memo dates, and the
attachment relationship. The cited discussion draft and testimony are not
included as separate documents in this packet.

Verification and limits
-----------------------
All 24 selected groups and body keys were checked against all 400 frozen pilot
records before retrieval. Twenty-one exact retained PDFs / 116 pages were
verified and rendered. The three other groups have only saved HTTP 404 error
responses; no attempt was made to treat those bodies as documents. They remain
explicitly unavailable in the receipt. All requests read retained objects;
no publisher acquisition was performed.

The unchanged linker now reports 60 reviewed documents / 329 pages and zero
mismatches. All original ledger fields except content_review remain unchanged,
including all 91 metadata decisions. Sixty have linked assistant reviews and
31 do not. All bound inputs and outputs were rechecked, as were the nine frozen
source hashes. No runtime code changed; no new test-suite result is claimed.
