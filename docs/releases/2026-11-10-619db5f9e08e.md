# November 10, 2026 — archive 619db5f9e08e

The archive adds 731 documents from the capture backlog, 22,217 historical committee roster seats and
the cleaned-up witness names from hearing prints, and it un-fuses documents that an earlier rule had
joined by mistake. It is the first release built and published by the nightly pipeline end to end.

[Published data manifest](https://congressional-data.spicygov.ai/viewer-publications/releases/619db5f9e08e813aa3ba474435cf9d158bd9eba9bdd14d2e619f55ee5b3cbfc0/manifest.json)

## What changed

- **New documents.** 731 documents: 718 pages and files from the capture runs of 2026-10-30/31 and
  2026-11-08, and 13 recovered copies (twelve Wayback copies, ct-l7uh, and one Duke copy, ct-umtm).
  945 link keys and 2,377 lookup keys are new; none was removed.
- **Documents no longer fused (ct-h3jb).** Files that a committee lists together count as one document
  only when they share a file name. Three State Department human-rights reports that were hidden behind
  a fourth are published again, a PowerPoint is separate from the testimony it sat beside, and a press
  page is separate from the PDF it was attached to. Every earlier link key, lookup key and address still
  answers the same file, the same document, or its own file after a split.
- **File names (ct-f7hp).** A later fetch that only found bytes the archive already held no longer
  renames the document. 1,443 documents change name against the previous release: 1,354 gain an
  official House-style name, 28 are neutral and 61 lose a copy number or bill detail.
- **Witness names (ct-rg34, ct-79j1).** Organization-first and multi-word-surname lines in hearing
  prints are read correctly: 2,738 witness rows change, 2,655 of them for the better. 26 older rows are
  marked replaced by a better-sourced row of the same person.
- **Historical rosters (ct-8cno).** 22,217 roster seats for Congresses 113 to 118.
- **Meetings.** 13 meetings are new; 18 gain a transcript; five hearing prints are linked to their
  meeting for the first time.
- **Videos.** 33 new videos and the recordings of 9 new meetings. About 74,000 video, channel and
  recording-match rows change only in the cache-file reference they cite (weekly refresh, ct-syp1).

## Known defects in this release

Each is recorded in the cause record as an accepted defect with its ticket.

- **54 testimonies labelled as biographies or support documents (ct-yuf0).** The House repository
  files them under a "Bio" or support-document name; the committee's own name says testimony, and the
  thirteen that were read are testimony. The file and every link to it are correct; the name and kind
  are not.
- **Two different testimonies under one document (ct-u9y4).** "Bowman Testimony.pdf" holds Terry
  Bowman's 2012 Oversight testimony and his 2018 Education and Workforce testimony as one document;
  this release shows the 2018 file where the previous one showed the 2012 file. 215 documents hold
  several different recovered files.
- **A help page as a meeting document (ct-aoxp).** `docs.house.gov/committee/Help.aspx` is listed as a
  document of 81 meetings.
- **Witness rows (ct-6mvl, ct-wy4c).** On 21 rows the organization sits in the position field; one
  reviewed blank position is filled wrongly; 299 rows no longer show a military rank or credential.
- **Rosters (ct-sh1i).** 19 seats of departed members of the 115th Congress carry no departure label;
  697 seats carry a rank the source does not state; the 118th joins three sources.
- **Titles (ct-ccsi).** 111 new documents are titled with a bare number, 163 with "Biography".
