# November 8, 2026 — archive 630164505301

The archive adds the historical hearing-to-video matcher results and corrects Senate Banking
hearing links, four witness records, one meeting subject and one recovered document. Congress
Explorer 0.6.4 follows the new release; the hosted service is re-pinned to it.

[Published data manifest](https://congressional-data.spicygov.ai/viewer-publications/releases/63016450530114d8ebc03d2936dc8293250ffd546bf00d5205909345216e5e78/manifest.json)

## What changed

- **Historical recording matches (ct-gi01).** New table `historical_recording_matches`: the retired
  Explorer's 34,641 package-level match observations, copied exactly from the October snapshot.
  16,141 printed hearings carry at least one video. Blank scores stay null; supplied zeros stay 0.
  These observations do not assign recordings to hearings or change meeting coverage.
- **Senate Banking hearings (ct-082q).** Hearing pages keep the publisher's date across saved
  copies and attach their witness roster through the exact saved page. 440 documents gain a hearing,
  25 move to the correct one, and 177 witness rows are corrected.
- **Witness records (ct-x78c).** A letter and a written-response heading no longer appear as
  people, and two organizations are corrected.
- **Meeting subject (ct-yci9).** House markup 115732 no longer lists HR 4775, which came only from
  an attachment filename.
- **Recovered document (ct-rie2).** One USAID document from a recovery cohort, with new
  `capture_*` columns in `url_state` that record each URL's recovery decision.

## Known limits

- 64 Banking witness keys from 5d24699ad9fa no longer resolve. They pointed at the wrong hearings;
  the owner accepted this on 2026-11-08.
- The Banking check cannot tell a wrong hearing on the same day as the right one; an independent
  reviewer read all 28 such pages by hand.
