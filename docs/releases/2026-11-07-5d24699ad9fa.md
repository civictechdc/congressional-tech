# November 7, 2026 — Congress Explorer 0.6.2

Congress Explorer now provides a dated activity feed, detailed votes and member
ballots, hearing dossiers, and citations that reopen the selected source passage.
The archive also corrects document associations, witness records, dates and
caption coverage.

This release follows the November 5 archive, `e145d24c389e`, and MCP 0.6.1.

`5d24699ad9fa076e3e10c894a1cb3310ab35b12e8025adcca87feca7978080c1`.

## Research tools

- **Follow legislation and individual votes.** Query 932,877 bill actions,
  30,702 roll calls and 10,561,417 member ballots with their original source
  references. Vote records remain available when their bills are outside the
  local bill collection. Ordering and paging preserve separate source events.
- **Find what happened during a week.** `get_activity` combines six supported
  event types, with chamber, committee and topic filters. It preserves supplied
  times, unknown times, and the distinction between an empty interval and
  unavailable coverage. Named research queries also cover bills, nominations
  and hearings.
- **Gather a hearing's sources.** `get_hearing_bundle` brings together witness
  records, testimony, transcripts, recordings, captions and coverage.
  `get_transcript` distinguishes official prints, committee transcripts and
  captions, and supports source selection and bounded page or cue reads.
- **Cite and reopen a passage.** References identify the release, saved source
  and page, text location or caption time. `resolve_citation` checks those
  details and reports changed, unavailable or unsupported witness attribution.
  Some source-specific acceptance remains open, as described below.

## Archive accuracy and browsing

- **Witnesses retain their source context.** Reviewed corrections repair names,
  roles and employers, and preserve explicitly represented organizations.
  Reviewed printed-material and address entries no longer appear as people. Separate
  roles remain separate, and a multi-day print's roster does not establish
  attendance at every sitting. Supported historical witness links continue to
  resolve; uncertain mappings remain explicit.
- **Publisher details survive extraction.** Documents retain descriptions,
  source links, publication and update dates, selected text and partial-reading
  outcomes. The release adds 1,125 committee records, including 31 from the
  selected Rules XML. Nomination records retain committee activity chronology.
- **Dates and bill references use supporting evidence.** Unsupported dates from
  descriptive addresses become unknown. Reviewed PDF covers correct or withhold
  bill and package identifiers. Party labels retain the publisher's value and
  leave missing values unknown.
- **Recordings and captions stay connected.** Event associations remain distinct
  from related printed packages. Caption tracks are separated from segments and
  placeholders; retained resources, source labels and missing material stay
  visible beside the recording they belong to.
- **Pages remain useful when optional data fails.** A failed supporting request
  leaves the core document or hearing available. Pasted source addresses can
  match equivalent space and percent-escape spellings after an exact lookup
  misses. The main navigation now opens the shared Congress Explorer; older
  meeting bookmarks redirect where a supported mapping exists.

## Processing and reliability

PDF readers retain physical page locations and stop decoding when the request
times out, is cancelled or its owner exits. Large saved bodies use bounded,
file-backed extraction and caching.

Publication can resume from verified completed work. Upload retries check
retained parts, metadata work has explicit budgets, and archive jobs account for
disk space and clean up their temporary data. House source processing preserves
malformed-input outcomes. The ballot export now writes all 10,561,417 rows within
the existing footer limit. Shared parser dependencies participate in reading
version decisions so retained results do not silently become stale.

## Known limits and remaining work

- Cross-document body and caption search still requires the deferred text index.
  Metadata discovery and supported reads within an individual document remain
  available.
- Supply a vote or member filter for ballot queries. Unfiltered hosted ballot
  pages can exceed the 10-second query deadline; keyed lookups passed the live
  checks. Broad-query performance tuning remains separate work.
- Three historical affiliation fields remain incorrect: Ella Wong-Ruskinko's
  recipient wording appears as a position, and Cindy McKim's and Victor Joseph's
  employer text includes separate people's entries. These records remain scoped
  to their printed packages. Senator Smith's identity and entry type remain
  unresolved. Twenty-one documents lack enough evidence for an exact
  role-specific witness association. [Remaining witness work](../../.tickets/ct-x78c.md)
- Transcript and citation acceptance remains open for selected sources,
  including two witness-attributed PDF reads that timed out.
  [Transcript scope](../../.tickets/ct-4kuo.md) ·
  [Citation scope](../../.tickets/ct-28tj.md)
- Some publisher-page hearing associations and attachment coverage remain
  incomplete. Wider HTML maintenance, automated-capture cutover and legacy
  retirement remain separate work.
  [Publisher-page scope](../../.tickets/ct-082q.md)
