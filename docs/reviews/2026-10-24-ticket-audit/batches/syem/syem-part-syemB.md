## Cross-ticket patterns
1. **Unverified audit evidence.** No proposal reproduced a failure or measured a cost. The headlines are LOC counts and labels. At least 9 specific claims were false or stale:
   - pytest-archon
   - the youtube cycle
   - the "none" caching bug
   - P1D parsing
   - runtime asserts
   - argv defaults
   - the missing README
   - the dead engine endpoints
   - the in-memory `at_url` fix

   Implementers then "fixed" these. In ct-elhm the fix made error handling worse.
2. **Decomposition by re-export shim.** Every split left a compatibility shim that production code still imports: `docspec_client/docspec.py`, `retention/docspec.py`, `retention/document_index.py` (with a `__setattr__` hack) and `news_lake docspec_backend.py`. There are also importer-less facade subpackages. Code moved; coupling did not change.
3. **Bundled mega-tickets closed by commit title.** Two tickets went into each of commits e4b3216b and 932933d1, each ticket carrying 10 to 25 items. Commit bodies are empty and no ticket has per-AC evidence. Titles overstate the work ("unify releases", "break cycles").
4. **"Wire the staged modules" produced unsafe or broken production paths.** streamed-recovery raises TypeError; export-pins writes a dangling live pointer. Their tests assert key names or call shapes, so they cannot see either failure.
5. **Duplication "fixed" by copying or partial delegation**: the youtube models, and only some of the `digest` and `encoded` helpers.
