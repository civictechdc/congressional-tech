## 2. Risks of reuse

- **Writer-supplied evidence.** `stored_sha256`, `stored_bytes` and `saved` were all produced by the same process that wrote the bytes. The size agreement with the listing is internally consistent by construction. Only reading the body checks it. The 198,676 PDFs where two different tools agree (the capture EOF check and pypdf) are the strongest borrowed values.
- **Pointer leakage.** The old index copies a line's outcome onto every capture row of that line, including `prior_attempts` and archive members. Borrowing `intact` is safe only from the final-content pointers (`["content","body"]`, `["provider_response","httpResponseBody"]`), which is the rule applied above. 30,712 member PDFs carry `saved` that belongs to their ZIP.
- **Code time-boundary.** The EOF check landed at `43f112ef` (2026-10-01 13:42Z), and the first 10-01 run started at 13:54Z. No older line records `writer.commit`, so the 15,475 PDFs that rest on 10-01 lines alone cannot be tied to that code.
- **Stale or moving versions.** Old readings span 12 parser fingerprints. House-naming is labeled 3.2.7 in both places, but the code changed afterwards. The old `readable_pdf` reader version differs from the one phase 1 will pin.
- **Same name, different meaning.**
  - Old `body_format` vs. DM `format` (zip/docx; html prefix-only; xml only House records).
  - Old `record_role` (`error-response`, `source-record`) vs. DM roles.
  - Old `filename` vs. DM effective filename.
  - Index `resolution` vs. DM `outcome`.
  - Old `content_document_kind` comes from PDF covers, which DM does not read.
- **Pre-deduplicated counts.** DM §6.3's 1,392,681 pairs and 331,985 empty-URL rows reproduce the old catalog exactly. The real work list is unknown until captures exist with DM's filename rule.

- A full JSON parse of the receipts: I ran substring scans only. The link and witness counts are line counts, not body counts.
- House XML, Senate pages and MODS fetched under the `documents/*` families. Family counts are a lower bound until the meeting-source registry exists.
- DM's effective-filename pairs, and so the true gap for job 6.
- Whether the 1,295 listed bodies with no capture row are real orphans or come from receipts the index dropped.
