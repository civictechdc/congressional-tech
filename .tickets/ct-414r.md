# The House page-date fallback links videos to the wrong meeting (about 65 live)

Found 2026-11-18 from the removed match that stopped that night (run 44326289806): match 38a1cd0bf65d linked event 117496 (House Transportation hearing of 2024-07-09 on the CARB in-use locomotive regulation) to youtube:PYk8qVbLMfA, whose own title is 'Roundtable on Reinvigorating the U.S.-Flag Fleet and Shipbuilding Industry'. It was published in eb6710bb1a14 on 2026-11-17, the day the roundtable's page (transportation.house.gov/calendar/EventSingle.aspx?EventID=407638) was first saved. The hearing has its own correctly titled recording, youtube:XP6tNikq9mg. The 2026-11-18 candidate removes the false link; that removal is correct.

Root-cause analysis by Codex (gpt-6.2-astra, xhigh, greybeard + etl-workflow): .cache/archive-release/investigations/false-page-match-20261118/codex-rca.md.
- model/sql/house-page-meeting-links.sql admits a page to a meeting of the same committee and date on two shared title words, two shared full-text words of the same kind, or one shared word when the meeting kind and the page are each alone that day. A roundtable and a hearing are the same kind (both are not markups). The page title and address share no word with the hearing title, so a word in the page's opening text admitted it. Which rule won is not kept, so this is inferred.
- Measured on eb6710bb1a14: 10,422 committee_page matches; both the video's and the meeting's title are present for 7,677; 978 of those share no substantive word (House 977 of 7,636). By Codex's reading of the titles at least 65 are genuinely false (conflicting named proceedings, event IDs or dates); many of the rest are true (abbreviations, 'Business Meeting'). That count is its judgment, not re-derived.
- Interaction with ct-mwod (pull request 201, one page under two host aliases is one page): if the page that broke uniqueness is an alias of the roundtable page, that fix would restore this false link. The first publish after 201 merges shows it: removed_keys either still names 38a1cd0bf65d or does not.

Options, not yet chosen: (a) the video's own title must not name a different proceeding than the meeting (cheap: videos.title is already a model input; Codex estimates 5 removals of clear roundtable-against-hearing pairs, none true); (b) the one-word fallback needs a word from the page title, not the opening text; (c) keep the winning rule and its words on the match so the next removal explains itself (etl-workflow verdict). Measure each against the 978 before choosing.

## Acceptance Criteria

The rule that admitted each committee_page match is kept with the match; the 978 no-shared-word pairs are classified; a chosen rule removes the false ones without losing matches judged true, with a test for a roundtable page on a hearing's date.
