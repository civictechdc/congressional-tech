# The capture backfill's sampling loop can lose a stop signal; one shared signal recorder for both loops

Found 2026-11-10 by the audit and the independent review of the watchdog signal fix (ct-psb5; evidence .cache/archive-release/investigations/docspec-feedback-20261110/refactor/review-watchdog-signal/rv_probe.py and rv_probe.out). packages/congress_api/src/congress_api/lake/capture_backfill.py:205 installs a signal handler that raises into whatever the main thread is running, around the same memory-sampling loop as scripts/archive_watchdog.py. It has the watchdog's old hazard (an exception raised just after subprocess takes a child's wait lock outside a try leaves the lock held, and the next wait on that child blocks for ever), and one more: the standard library's selector swallows an InterruptedError raised inside it (selectors.py:416 in CPython 3.12), so a signal that arrives during a select is lost outright; the reviewer reproduced that. Other handlers that raise, of a different kind (SIGALRM deadlines, which have the same class of hazard but are not sampling loops): packages/congress_api/src/congress_api/parsers/body_text.py:170, parsers/html_metadata.py:77, apps/explorer_mcp/src/congressional_explorer_mcp/pdf_decoder.py:169. One handler does not raise but calls Event.set() inside the handler: packages/congress_api/src/congress_api/cli/house_sites.py:37. Wanted end state: one recording handler (it records the signal and returns; the loop acts at a safe point) in scripts/measure/process_rss.py, used by the watchdog and by the backfill loop, instead of a second fix written out again. Not started; the watchdog's own fix is on claude/dx-watchdog-signal.

## Acceptance Criteria

A test that plants the lost signal in the backfill loop (the signal delivered during the sampler's select) and fails before the fix. The backfill loop and the watchdog use one shared recording handler; a signal at any moment stops the backfill's job within a stated bound and is reported. The SIGALRM deadline handlers are each either shown safe at the points where they can fire or replaced by a deadline that does not raise into arbitrary code; any that touch a byte-hashed parser file are listed, not changed, with the coordinator's decision recorded. Independent review by someone who reads the signal handling; one full lane or all six shards.
