# Filename reader takes 38 'Truth in Testimony' names for testimony, so 9 right TTF names give way to them

Found 2026-11-10 measuring the name rule (ct-h8dr) on release 619db5f9e08e. Of 323 committee file names that say Truth in Testimony, the filename reader gives 282 the disclosure family and 38 (27 distinct names, on 27 published documents) the statement family: run-together or misspelt forms such as 'Web_Hedren_TruthinTestimony04.09.19.pdf', '20160907HL-Truthi-in-Testimony-Wynn.pdf', 'truth_in_testimomy_slater_Copy.pdf' and 'Taylor Testimony.Truth in Testimony.pdf'. Under the name rule a House TTF name beside such a committee name counts as contradicted and gives way: 9 of the 19 TTF names that give way go to a name that itself says Truth in Testimony (3 read: all three are the disclosure form). List: .cache/archive-release/investigations/name-rule-20261110/nr_35_known_wrong_giveways.out and nr_36_ttf_reader.out. The reader is the byte-hashed house_naming package, so the fix is a parser version change, not model SQL.

## Acceptance Criteria

The reader gives every name that says Truth in Testimony (any spacing, the common misspellings) the disclosure family, with a recorded parser version change. On the next build the 9 documents show their House TTF name again and contradicted_official_saved_names stays 0.
