# The 8,192-row bounded-memory test sits on the pinned engine's edge

Found 2026-11-10 while reproducing the hosted failures of the engine's one line (ct-x00f); measured through the engine's command line on the retained job of packages/congress_api/tests/test_capture_comparison_memory.py::test_full_comparison_preserves_file_references_and_restorations_at_four_times_rows[8192] (evidence: .cache/archive-release/investigations/docspec-feedback-20261110/refactor/one-line/ol_full8192_repeats.tsv and ol_full8192_memory_sweep.tsv). The test runs under limited_engine: memory_bytes 192 MiB, compute_threads 2, batch_rows 32, max_batch_bytes 1 MiB, max_metadata_bytes 1 MiB. On the PINNED engine (b0342a7): passes 5 of 5 at 2 threads; fails 5 of 5 at 4 threads at the same limits ('Failed to reserve memory for sort during spill', step tagged_facts); in a sweep of the memory limit at 2 threads it fails at 176 MiB and passes at 192 MiB. So the limit the test asserts is the smallest passing value of the sweep: its margin over the pinned engine's real need is under 16 MiB (8 percent), and its outcome depends on the thread count, which the test fixes at 2 for that reason (its own comment says every sort partition reserves memory independently). A test meant to bound the engine's memory therefore also fails on any engine change that moves the need by a few MiB in either direction of accounting, with a message that does not say by how much. It is doing useful work today: it caught a real increase on the engine's one line (needs 256 MiB there, from metadata charges on the engine's own spill files, being fixed on the engine side). Do not change its limits as part of that engine work.

## Acceptance Criteria

The test states what it bounds and by how much: the measured smallest passing limit for the pinned engine at 2 threads is recorded beside the assertion (with the date and engine revision), and the asserted limit is that minimum plus a stated headroom, or the test asserts the minimum directly by a small search and compares it with a recorded bound, so a failure reports 'needs N MiB, bound M MiB' instead of a spill error. The thread dependence is either tested on purpose (a second bound at 4 threads) or said in the test's name. No limit is raised to make an engine change pass without the before and after need being measured and written down.
