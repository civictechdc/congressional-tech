# validate_docmeta_v4 accepts a usable document with no primary_kind

Measured 2026-11-17 on completed_no_llm_v6_2.parquet (64,936 rows): the sidecar builder's own strict validator (scripts/build_v6_2_ssot_sidecar.py) and congress_docmeta.validate_docmeta_v4 agree on every real row and on six planted fault kinds, but only the builder's rejects a usable document whose primary_kind was removed (753 of 3,247 dropped-key cases; its error is usable_missing_primary_kind). The sweep writes strict_v4_valid from validate_docmeta_v4, and the release's docmeta_v6_1 invariants trust that flag. Two validators for one schema is also the duplication the owner asked to remove.

## Acceptance Criteria

One validator; a usable document without primary_kind is invalid; the sweep and the sidecar builder both call it; the planted-fault comparison is kept as a test.
