# Executive business meeting wording

Decision: expose the full printed meeting heading rather than retaining only
Business Meeting or a generic name containing EBM.

Discovery: the fixed 333,368-name corpus includes 184 filenames containing
Executive Business wording and 150 containing separated EBM. The latter all
come from Judiciary Senate URLs. A wider 733-name executive/EBM inventory is
retained before edits. The current reader already extracts Results, ordinary
Business Meeting labels, dates and Executive Session wording separately.

Hypothesis: one supplemental wording rule can expose Executive Business Meeting(s)
and preserve EBM as an unexpanded abbreviation when it occurs inside an already
recognized results heading. Full wording may have explicit Open/Closed wording;
none of these fields verifies access, occurrence or a result. Do not expand EBM
from the filename alone or classify generic EBM prose as a meeting.

Compare the accepted congress-boundaries output, the frozen native/strict parser
and the candidate on identical inputs. Require every prior complete output to
remain exact after removing only the reviewed new observations. Reuse existing
comparison/review harnesses and the one existing strict correction expectation.

Inspect retained download receipts and original PDF text for Judiciary and Budget
examples. Exercise explicit phrases, results abbreviations, adjacent numeric
suffixes, percent escapes, protected witness IDs and nonmeeting negative controls.
Run focused tests, regression tests, full comparison, typed-adapter equivalence,
corpus checks and the native omission audit. Retain failed trials and source
hashes. Each full run is bounded to 30 minutes; no new dependencies/downloads.
