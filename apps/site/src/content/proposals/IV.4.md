Extract and chart historical committee funding levels from House/Senate funding resolutions to show trends over time.

## Problem

Information on the funding levels allocated to House and Senate committees is not readily accessible or easily tracked over time.

## Solution

Locate and extract data from committee funding resolutions passed by the House and Senate for each Congress. These resolutions typically follow a formula. Compile the extracted data into a spreadsheet, going back as far as possible, and develop visualizations to show funding trends over time.
